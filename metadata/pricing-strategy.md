# Pricing Strategy - Lexingo AI

## Launch Strategy: Freemium Model

### Free Tier Features:
- PDF upload and reading (up to 5 documents)
- Basic word translation (100 translations/day)
- Text-to-speech (limited usage)
- Basic reading progress tracking

### Premium Subscription - "Lexingo Pro"

#### Monthly Plan: $9.99 USD
- Unlimited PDF uploads and storage
- Unlimited word and paragraph translations
- AI-powered chat with contextual learning
- Advanced text-to-speech with multiple voices
- Speech-to-text functionality
- Reading analytics and progress insights
- Priority support
- Offline translation cache

#### Annual Plan: $79.99 USD (33% savings)
- All monthly features included
- 2 months free compared to monthly billing
- Early access to new features

## Pricing Tiers by Market:

### Tier 1 Markets (USD baseline):
- United States, Canada, Australia, UK, Germany
- Monthly: $9.99 | Annual: $79.99

### Tier 2 Markets (Adjusted):
- Spain, France, Italy, Japan
- Monthly: $8.99 | Annual: $69.99

### Tier 3 Markets (Lower pricing):
- Latin America, Eastern Europe, India
- Monthly: $4.99 | Annual: $39.99

## Family Sharing:
- Premium subscription can be shared with up to 6 family members
- Each member gets individual progress tracking
- Centralized billing through family organizer

## Educational Discounts:
- 50% discount for verified students
- 40% discount for educational institutions
- Bulk licensing available for schools

## Launch Promotion:
- **First month free** for early adopters
- **50% off first year** for first 1000 subscribers
- **Referral program:** 1 month free for each successful referral

## Price Change Policy:
- 30 days advance notice for existing subscribers
- Grandfathered pricing for annual subscribers during their term
- Clear communication through in-app notifications and email

## Revenue Projections:

### Conservative Estimates:
- 1000 active users after 3 months
- 5% conversion rate to premium
- 50 paying subscribers × $9.99 = $499.50/month

### Optimistic Estimates:
- 5000 active users after 6 months
- 10% conversion rate to premium
- 500 paying subscribers × $9.99 = $4,995/month

## Platform Fees:
- Apple App Store: 30% (first year), 15% (subsequent years)
- RevenueCat: 1% of revenue
- Net revenue: ~69% (first year), ~84% (subsequent years)

## Future Pricing Considerations:
- Premium+ tier with advanced AI features: $19.99/month
- Corporate/Business plans: Custom pricing
- API access for developers: Usage-based pricing

## Price Testing Strategy:
1. Launch with current pricing
2. A/B test different price points after 3 months
3. Survey users on willingness to pay
4. Adjust based on conversion data and user feedback

## Competitive Analysis:
- Duolingo Plus: $6.99/month
- Babbel: $13.95/month
- Rosetta Stone: $11.99/month
- **Our positioning:** Premium AI-powered tool, priced competitively in middle range
